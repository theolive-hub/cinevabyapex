      The wall comes to you.

      By Cineva crew · June 13, 2026

      Your stage is already rented and your sets are already standing. Cineva by Apex Sound & Light builds the LED volume into a corner of that same room, runs it for as long as the show needs, and strikes it when you are done.

      That is the whole advantage. The wall sits beside your other sets, on floor you are already paying for, so there is no separate virtual production stage, no company move, and no second location on the call sheet.

      Not a facility

      A lot of virtual production gets sold as real estate: a separate room you rent by the day with a fixed wall in it, that your unit has to travel to. That is not what we do. We are not a stage you book. We bring the volume to the stage you already have and set it up alongside your practical sets, so the LED work and everything else happen under one roof, on one call sheet, with no company move.

      Sized to the shot

      Because we build the volume fresh in your space, it is sized to the corner you can give it and the scene in front of you, not to a room someone fit out two years ago. Need it for three days or ten months? Same answer. You are using floor you have already paid for, and the schedule never leaves the building.

      And one crew owns the whole thing: they build it, run it, and strike it. The knowledge from prep walks straight onto your floor instead of being handed off to strangers who happen to work the building.

      What you get

      The full build comes as one delivery: walls, ceilings, the processor, scaffold and rigging, driving-plate and media-server playback, specialty lighting, and the crew to run all of it. One call, one team, one PO, scoped and scheduled around your shoot. How turnkey delivery works.

      How it works

      Tell us the shot, the stage or location, and the dates. We scope the build, set the pre-light and load-in around your schedule, and put the wall on your floor ready for camera. A call at greenlight buys the calmest version of that week.

      The point is simple: you do not move to the wall. The wall comes to you.

      Shooting somewhere already?

      Tell us where you are shooting and what the scene needs. We bring the LED volume, the playback, and the crew to your set, sized to the shot, and tell you straight what the build takes. You stay put; the wall travels.
