Common questions

Quebec shoots.

Do you have a Montreal location?

No. Cineva is based in Pickering, Ontario, and we travel to Quebec. Our travel kit brings the LED volume, processing, playback, lighting, and crew to your Montreal stage or location.

Do we need to book a Montreal stage?

No. If you have a stage or location in Quebec with your sets in it, we bring the LED volume there and build it in the room you are already using, sized to the shot. You keep the unit together and skip a move to a separate facility.

What do you bring to a Quebec shoot?

Walls, ceilings, processing, scaffold and rigging, media-server playback, specialty lighting, and the crew to build and run it. One call, one team, delivered to your set.
