# Cineva — Hero Image Ideas

One brief per page. Each lists a primary concept plus a backup. All shots should read **set-facing, not showroom** — real gear, real crews, available light plus LED glow. Cyan/stage accent should appear naturally in the frame (LED wash, monitor glow, gel) rather than be added in post. Wide, cinematic crops with room for the headline on the left or lower third. No glossy stock, no smiling people pointing at screens.

---

*"On set. On time. On budget." / LED Volumes & Virtual Production*

## frame-remapping.html — Frame Remapping

- **Backup:** Operator at a monitor showing the remapped frustum on the wall, the raw wall pattern visible behind it — the "what the camera sees vs. what's really there" contrast.

### Production notes
- **Aspect:** shoot wide (16:9 / 21:9) with safe space on the left third for headline + CTA.
- **Color:** let stage cyan and tungsten warm do the work in-camera; keep grades filmic, not neon.
- **People:** crews working, not models posing. Faces optional — silhouettes and hands read just as well.
- **Consistency:** same haze/contrast treatment across all heroes so the site feels like one shoot, even if sourced separately.
