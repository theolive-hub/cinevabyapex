Frame Remapping

Frame Remapping.
Every camera sees its own perspective.

Multi-camera frame remapping rig

Multiple genlocked cameras, each seeing the LED wall from its own correct perspective — not the wide shot's perspective, not a compromise. Interleaved frames for keying when the shot demands it.

This is the technical bridge between virtual production and multi-camera coverage. Without frame remapping, your B-camera sees the A-camera's parallax. With it, every angle is correct. The background shifts naturally as the camera moves, because the system knows where each camera is.

We set it up. We prove it in prep. It works on the day. Frame remapping is one of those things that's invisible when it's right — and ruins the shot when it's wrong. We make sure it's right.

Common questions

Before you spec the remap.

How many cameras can you remap simultaneously?

Multi-camera setups supported — each camera sees its own correct perspective on the wall.

Does this work with interleaved frames for keying?

Yes. Frames can be interleaved between camera angles when chroma-key compositing is in the workflow.

What's the latency?

Frame-accurate. Sync to camera is the design constraint, not an afterthought.
