About

Thirty-six years
on Canada's
working sets.

Cineva by Apex Sound & Light is the set-facing AV and specialty lighting arm of Apex Sound & Light — a 36-year production house based in Toronto, Ontario. Where Apex serves the full spectrum of professional AV, Cineva exists for one audience: film and television.

The name comes from the work. Cineva builds the LED volumes, runs the driving plates, frame-remaps the multi-camera shoot, drives the playback servers — pre-prepped on the bench, calibrated for camera, dispatched on time.

We created Cineva because the film and TV community deserves a partner who speaks their language. Not a rental catalog. Not a sales pitch. A crew that understands call times, turnaround days, and what happens when the AD calls "rolling" and the wall isn't ready.

The warehouse

Where every order starts.

Every piece of kit ships from our warehouse. Gear is bench-tested, firmware-checked, cable-counted, case-labeled, and manifested before it rolls. When it arrives on your stage, it's ready. Not "should be ready." Ready.

The four pillars

What "ready" means to us.

Checked

Every item is bench-tested by an AV tech who has done the job on set. Not a warehouse pull — a prep. Power-on, signal-through, firmware verified, accessories confirmed. The tech who tests it signs the manifest. If it fails on set, that's on us. It doesn't fail on set.

Clean

Calibrated. Colour-checked. Genlocked. Gaff-taped where tape belongs. Cables dressed, connectors seated, dust covers on. The gear arrives looking like someone cares — because someone does. A dirty flight case tells your gaffer everything they need to know about the vendor. Ours are clean.

Labeled

Every case tagged. Every cable counted. Manifest in the truck. Your best boy doesn't spend the first hour of the day figuring out which road case has the spare processor. It's labeled, it's on the manifest, and the manifest matches the PO. Count it in, count it out.

Supported

The prep tech who staged your order is the one who answers at 02:00. Not a call centre. Not a voicemail tree. The person who packed the case, tested the signal chain, and knows which backup unit is already in your B-truck. On-set support is 24/7 because sets don't stop at 18:00.
