LED video, specialty lighting, and AV solutions for film and TV.

Cineva is the film and television arm of Apex Sound & Light, built to keep production moving.

We bring the right equipment, crew, and set sense to fast-moving shoot days. Shots change, departments overlap, we bring the experience and knowledge to move with the pace, and keep the tech as invisible as possible.
