LED Volumes

Cineva builds LED volumes from the ground up — walls, ceilings, LED processor, scaffold, rigging. Every build is film-grade: sized to the shot, calibrated for the camera system you're running, and prepped on the bench before it ships. We don't rent panels and wish you luck. We build the stage, test it, and hand you a volume that's ready for first unit.

Driving Plates

Playback systems engineered for car-on-stage work. We build around your workflow — brightness matched to your LED wall, colour calibrated to your camera's colour science, frame-accurate sync so the plate tracks the rig. No guesswork at the monitor. The plate plays when the AD calls action.

Frame Remapping

Multiple genlocked cameras, each seeing the LED wall from its own correct perspective — not the wide shot's perspective, not a compromise. Interleaved frames for keying when the shot demands it. This is the technical bridge between virtual production and multi-camera coverage. We set it up. We prove it in prep. It works on the day.

Multi-camera frame remapping setup
Per-camera perspective correction Genlocked multi-cam Interleaved frames for keying Proven in prep

Server Playback

Media servers for plates, environment loops, multi-wall sync. DIT-compatible colour pipeline from ingest to output. We run the servers so you can focus on the shot — content loaded, cues set, timecode locked. When the DP asks for a different sky, it's a server recall, not a re-render.
