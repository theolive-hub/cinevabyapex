Common questions

Before you make the call.

What does "turnkey" cover?

Everything from first call to wrap. Pre-shoot consulting, equipment prep, crew, transport, and on-set support for the duration. One contact, one invoice, no gaps between who owns what.

Do you provide operators and crew?

Yes. Programmers, lighting techs, server operators, and riggers. The tech who preps your order at the warehouse is the same one standing next to you on set. No handoffs, no strangers.

Do you work on union shoots?

Yes. We're frequent partners with NABET, IATSE, and Teamsters crews. Our team knows set protocol and we build around your collective agreements.

How early should I bring you in?

As soon as the show is greenlit. The earlier we're in the room, the better the build. Last-minute works too, but early is where we do our best work for you.
