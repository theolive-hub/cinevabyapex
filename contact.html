Common questions

Before you call.

What's your typical lead time?

Cineva ships kit on the call sheet. Standard prep is 48 hours from quote sign-off. Rush turnarounds are available with notice — we'll tell you what's possible before you commit.

Where do you deliver?

Toronto sound stages and locations across the GTA. Travel kit and crew available for Vancouver, Montreal, and Atlanta with appropriate lead time.

Do you provide crew?

Yes. Turnkey Support covers labour, crew, and pre-shoot consulting. The prep tech who stages your order is the one who answers when something needs attention on set.

How are quotes priced?

Day rate for shoot days, prep day rate for pre-light. Transport, insurance, and labour quoted with the kit. We don't have hidden line items — what's on the quote is what's on the truck.

Can I visit the warehouse?

Yes, by appointment. 1845 Clements Rd in Pickering. Call ahead so the kit you want to see is on the bench when you arrive.
