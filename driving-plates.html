Driving Plates

Driving Plates.
Calibrated colour, frame-accurate sync.

Car-on-stage driving plate setup

Playback systems engineered for car-on-stage work. We build around your workflow — brightness matched to your LED wall, colour calibrated to your camera's colour science, frame-accurate sync so the plate tracks the rig.

No guesswork at the monitor. The plate plays when the AD calls action. The colour holds from the server to the wall to the sensor. The sync is tight enough that the plate moves with the car rig, not a frame behind it.

We've run driving plates on stages across the GTA — from single-wall setups to full wraps. Tell us the car, the stage, and the camera, and we'll build the playback system around it.

Common questions

Before you spec the plate.

Do you provide the plates or do we?

Either. We work with your captured plates or source from a stock library. Custom plate shoots available with notice.

How is sync maintained to camera?

Frame-accurate sync via timecode and genlock. The wall and the camera move on the same heartbeat.

What's the colour pipeline?

DIT-compatible, Rec.709 and Rec.2020 supported, ACES-ready.
