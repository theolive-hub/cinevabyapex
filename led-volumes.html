LED volume build on stage

Cineva by Apex Sound & Light builds LED volumes from the ground up — walls, ceilings, LED processor, scaffold, rigging. Every build is film-grade: sized to the shot, calibrated for the camera system you're running, and prepped on the bench before it ships.

We don't rent panels and wish you luck. We build the stage, test it, and hand you a volume that's ready for first unit. The processor is configured. The colour is matched. The genlock is proven. When the DP walks on stage and sees the environment for the first time, it works.

Our warehouse is where every volume starts. Panels are tested, firmware is current, spares are packed, and the entire rig is manifested before it rolls. The tech who builds it is the tech who answers at 02:00 if something goes sideways on set.

Common questions

Before you spec the wall.

What pixel pitch do you stock?

2.6mm, 2.9mm, and 3.9mm Black Pearl panels. The right pitch depends on closest camera distance to the wall — we'll spec on a walkthrough.

Do you provide the processor and genlock?

Yes. Brompton processor, genlock sync to camera, ColourScience HDR pipeline.

How long does it take to build a volume?

A small volume comes up in a day. A full virtual production stage takes 2–3 days of pre-light plus a load-in shift.
