Specialty Lighting Touring-grade kit, broadcast spec.

Moving lights, LED effects fixtures, hazers, and practicals. Touring-grade kit maintained to broadcast spec. We serve both lighting departments and art departments.

Need a window gag? A lightning rig? A wall of tube lights for a music video? The gear is prepped, the dimmer is hot-patched, the tech knows the console. We carry the fixtures, the control, the distribution, and the crew to run it.

Our inventory is maintained to touring and broadcast standard. Every fixture is tested, every lamp is logged, every cable is labelled. When it arrives on your stage, it's ready to patch and go.

Moving lights, camera-safe.

Our moving-light inventory runs deep: Robe, Ayrton, GLP, Chauvet, Elation, and Acme. Touring-grade fixtures, but specced for the camera, not just the eye. That means flicker-free drivers that hold up at high frame rate, accurate colour for skin tones, and dimming that stays clean on a calibrated pipeline. Spot, beam, wash, effects, run from a full suite of grandMA consoles with the programmer to drive them.

It is the same lane whether the job is a big night exterior, an arena-scale wash, or moving heads built into a music-video look. Touring-grade output, broadcast discipline. For what each fixture is for and how to pick one, read the moving-light inventory.

Hide a light anywhere.

For practicals and window gags, the wireless toolkit does the work: Astera Titan Tubes, NYX bulbs, and HydraPanels, battery-powered and app-controlled, so there is no cable run and no rigging compromise. If a light has to live inside the set or just out of frame, these go there.

Specialty lighting also works hand in hand with an LED volume: the wall throws the motivated source and the moving lights and practicals fill the rest of the frame. Spec it as part of a full turnkey build and one crew runs the lighting and the wall together.

Common questions

Before you spec the rig.

Do you stock Astera tubes and bulbs?

Yes. Titan Tube FP1, NYX Bulb FP5, and HydraPanel FP6. All battery-powered, wireless, full RGB+W, app-controlled over AirLink. No cable runs, no rigging compromises. If you need to hide a light somewhere, these go there.

What about atmospherics?

MDG and Reel EFX for film-set haze, engineered not to trigger smoke detectors and clean enough for ICVFX work. Ultratec and Antari for theatre and concert builds where you need heavier effect. We match the machine to the environment.

Can you build a full lighting package for a feature or series?

Yes. We spec, prep, and deliver complete packages out of our Pickering warehouse. Tell us your shoot days and we'll build a rig that fits the call sheet.

Do you supply lighting consoles?

Yes. Tell us what your programmer runs and we'll match the desk. We can provide the console alone or with a programmer if you need one.
