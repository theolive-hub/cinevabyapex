Moving lights and effects fixtures

Moving lights, LED effects fixtures, hazers, practicals — touring-grade kit maintained to broadcast spec. We serve both lighting departments and art departments.

Need a window gag? A lightning rig? A wall of tube lights for a music video? The gear is prepped, the dimmer is hot-patched, the tech knows the console. We carry the fixtures, the control, the distribution, and the crew to run it.

Our inventory is maintained to touring and broadcast standard. Every fixture is tested, every lamp is logged, every cable is labelled. When it arrives on your stage, it's ready to patch and go.
